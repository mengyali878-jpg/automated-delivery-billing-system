Recommended browser: Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
Supported diagnostic format:
state=PRESENT ... L/W/H(mm)=299/194/148 weight(g)=270 ...

International Rate Rules

This page is an offline prototype, not a live UPS, FedEx, USPS, or DHL rate API. It follows common U.S. dimensional/billable-weight logic and then applies local reference price bands for the selected carrier, service, ZIP/postal-code zone, and delivery type.

UPS / FedEx / DHL: dimensional weight uses rounded whole-inch dimensions and a 139 in³/lb divisor. Billable weight is the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight, rounded up to a whole pound.

USPS: this prototype applies USPS-style DIM weight only when the package exceeds 1 cubic foot (1,728 in³); otherwise billing is based on actual weight.
